The rules are in a 34-page PDF

Korea sorts household waste into more than a dozen streams. The Ministry of Environment's guide lists hundreds of items, and many look recyclable but aren't:

receiptseggshellsice packsoily pizza boxes

Of 75,000 t of paper cartons shipped in 2022, about 60% were mixed into waste paper and 27% went into garbage bags (Hankook Ilbo, 2024). A plain search box misses typos and English; a chatbot can't show its source.

How it works

316 entries from the Ministry of Environment guide, Seoul and a 2026 Q&A, each with its source page. Newer rules override older ones, and the old sentence stays visible.

Korean is split into jamo, so "껍떼기" still finds "껍질". TF-IDF over character n-grams, plus a check that every typed word is explained.

A tuned threshold decides: answer, show 3 candidates, or say "not in the guide".

Static PWA: works offline, no server, no API key.

Measuring it

Pilot: one dorm or apartment block, 4 weeks. Weeks 1–2 baseline, weeks 3–4 with a QR code to Bunri at the recycling area.

Measure contamination in recycling bags (same sample each week) and the on-device pilot log: lookups, items not in the guide, "still not sure".
