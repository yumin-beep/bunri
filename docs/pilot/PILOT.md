# 4-week building pilot

What it tests: whether putting Bunri one QR scan away at the recycling area lowers contamination in that building's recycling.

| Week | What happens | What is measured |
|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | Baseline. No posters. | Bag audit (audit_sheet.html), same weekday and time |
| 3–4 | A4 posters (poster.html) at the recycling area and elevator | Bag audit + Bunri's on-device pilot log |

**Main metric:** contamination rate = items that don't belong in that stream ÷ items checked, from 10 recycling bags a week chosen by position, not by look. Compared weeks 1–2 vs 3–4.

**Secondary:**
- Paper cartons in the paper pile. The carton rule changed in 2026 and is the most-missed rule in the national figures.
- Bunri pilot log, exported as CSV from the app's "Pilot log" button: lookups, "not in guide" queries (what to add next), "still not sure" taps.

**Cost:** printing 4 A4 sheets, about 30 minutes a week for one person to audit. No server, no accounts, nothing installed.

**Limits:** one building, 4 weeks, no control building, so a change can't be attributed to Bunri alone. The log counts lookups, not people. A second round would add a control building with posters but no QR.

**Privacy:** the pilot log stays on the device that made the lookups; nothing is sent anywhere. Bag audits count items, not households.
